## Page content

Name: Pebbles
Species: African Spurred Tortoise (Sulcata Tortoise)
About Pebbles: 25 years old, and 40 pounds of charm. He has been nicknamed “the Star of Autrey Mill” because of his big size and even bigger personality. He loves to sleep in and take laps around his outdoor enclosure. He loves basking in the sunshine and can be found hiding in his outdoor burrow. He particularly loves being sprayed with the hose on a hot day! Some of his favorite treats are collard greens, blueberries, and carrot. Mainly Pebbles eats Timothy Hay and grass. He is often the center of attention for various programs at Autrey Mill. If you request a Nature Birthday Bash, you could even have the chance to meet Pebbles!
