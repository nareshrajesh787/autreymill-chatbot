## Page content

Autrey Mill is a 46-acre preserve with 1.25 miles of trails, is free to visit; donations are appreciated. Bringing a Group of 10+, please make a reservation. Call 678-366-3511 or email info@autreymill.org
If you just plan on exploring the preserve, visiting the animals, and hiking the trails, then no reservation is necessary.
The Visitor Center houses over 30 different animals you can learn about and offers a unique educational experience, an area for kids to learn about the world around them, with little guided instruction.
Listen to our cell phone audio tour to enhance your visitor experience and learn about our historic buildings and features as you walk along the Heritage Village pathways and preserve trails. Instructions are posted on the main kiosk in the parking lot.
