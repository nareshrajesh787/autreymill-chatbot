## Page content

The Visitor Center and Farm Museum are open Tuesday through Saturday, 10 AM to 4 PM & Sunday, 12 PM to 4 PM. Trails: Open daily 8 AM to 9 PM.
Autrey Mill is a 46-acre preserve with 1.25 miles of trails, is free to visit; donations are appreciated. Bringing a Group of 10+, please make a reservation. Call 678-366-3511 or email info@autreymill.org
46 acres of forest, woodland trails, ravines, pollinator gardens, and historic buildings. Autrey Mill is a unique setting for weddings, receptions, anniversary celebrations, and more!
Over its history, the 46-acre Autrey Mill property has changed from dense forest to fertile farmland, and now back to woodlands, a city park & a historic village, preserving buildings to tell the story of the farm families that once lived nearby.

Autrey Mill Nature Preserve would like to extend a sincere and heartfelt end-of-year thank you to the residents of the Country Club of the South (CCS) and the CCS Charity Guild for your incredible support. The Country Club of the South Charity Guild’s generosity in 2025 supported campers in need from across our community: six from Johns Creek, two from Duluth, two from Peachtree Corners, four from Alpharetta, and one from Roswell. The support for our Summer Camp programs truly made a difference. We are deeply grateful for your commitment to our community.
We are deeply grateful to Veolia for their continued support of Autrey Mill Nature Preserve and their commitment to protecting our local waterways. Through their partnership, Veolia helps power our Johns Creek cleanup efforts throughout and enables us to offer meaningful educational experiences that connect kids and families to the Chattahoochee River watershed.

Hours

Visitor Center and Farm Musuem Open: Tuesday through Saturday from 10 am to 4 pm and Sunday 12 pm to 4 pm. Closed Mondays. The grounds, trails, and outdoor animals are able to be visited daily from 8 am to 9 pm.
Closed on major holidays and access may be restricted during events, programs, and summer camp.
It is free to visit. Donations are appreciated.
