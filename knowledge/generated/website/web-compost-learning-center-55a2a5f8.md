Compost Learning Center
Green Cell Inc.
COMPOST WORKSHOP
Looking for a way to responsibly take care of your household organic waste and reduce landfill? Come join Green Cell Inc. and Autrey Mill Nature Preserve Association to learn ‘How to Compost’. This interactive workshop will not only help you take the right steps to start composting but find a mentor to guide you through the process from start to finish.
Where:
Autrey Mill Nature Preserve – Upper Pavilion by the Warsaw Church
9770 Autrey Mill Road
Johns Creek, GA 30022
When:
Saturday, February 11th, 2023
10 AM – 12 PM
More information:
This workshop will be run by Green Cell Inc., a volunteer-run non-profit organization, passionate to raise awareness about human-created environmental issues and promoting conscious consumerism with a focus on individual and collective action in the community. Register Today
