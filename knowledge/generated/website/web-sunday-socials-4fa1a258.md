Sunday Socials
What is a Sunday Social?
A place for everyone to gather! Join heritage staff on the front porch of one of our historic buildings for an open house and participate as we make a vintage craft! Children are welcome but please note this is a “grown-up and me” experience.
1 pm – 3 pm
Offered the 1st and 3rd Sunday of the month of September-December (excluding holidays).
All ages are welcome, but children must be accompanied by a Grown-Up.
Registration is not required
Suggested Donation of $5
