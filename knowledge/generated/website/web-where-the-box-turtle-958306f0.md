## Page content

Name: Where
Species: Eastern Box Turtle
About: ‘Where’ is the box turtle that has the shell with the most square shape to it! He is the second-largest male and he has the largest beak out of all of the box turtles. His eyes are a very bright scarlet and he has some pretty orange patterns on his skin. He is often found stomping around the food bowl looking for his next snack. He has a big appetite to fit his big beak!
