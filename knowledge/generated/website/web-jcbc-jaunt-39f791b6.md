5k and Fun Run for AMNP!
This year we are honored to have been chosen as the beneficiary of the 2nd Annual JCBC Jaunt 5K and 1-Mile Fun Run, hosted by Johns Creek Baptist Church. JCBC created this community event as a way to bring neighbors together for fun, fitness, and giving back to the community, and we are thrilled that the proceeds will support the mission of Autrey Mill Nature Preserve.
Every registration helps us continue providing educational programs, caring for our animals, and preserving the history and natural spaces our community loves. Proceeds from the Jaunt will also help fund our camp scholarships for families in need and ensure every child has a place to belong, grow, and thrive outdoors.
