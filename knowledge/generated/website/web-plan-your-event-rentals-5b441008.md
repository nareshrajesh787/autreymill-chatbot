## Page content

46 acres of forest, woodland trails, ravines, pollinator gardens, and historic buildings. Autrey Mill is a unique setting for weddings, receptions, anniversary celebrations, and more!
The arrangement of our historic buildings forms a small Heritage Village, which makes a charming wedding venue, with a short stroll from a ceremony in the Old Warsaw Church to a reception in the Summerour House.
Rental fees are used to continue preserving Autrey Mill’s natural environment and history, provide educational programming, and operate the preserve. For our pricing brochure, please email us.

Please review our frequently asked questions!
How do I book a rental?
Please email the Sales Director and include your phone number, billing address, requested event date and time, event description (i.e. wedding, graduation party, reunion, etc…), anticipated number of guests, the names of the spaces you are interested in, and any special accommodations you might require. Your date is not reserved until the deposit is paid. Email: megan@autreymill.org.
Is a deposit required to book a rental?
Yes, an upfront non-refundable deposit of 25% of your total is due to reserve your date.
Can I tour the venue before booking?
Absolutely, but a reservation is required. Please email the rental Sales Director. Typically, tours are hosted during staff hours 10-4 Tuesday-Saturday. Please provide three times and dates that work for you. Email: megan@autreymill.org
What is the cancellation policy?
Your date is not reserved until the deposit is paid. The non-refundable deposit is retained by Autrey Mill in the event of a cancellation.
The Reservation Deposit is non-refundable;
Refunds of any additional rental payments:
Event rentals of $500 and more, no refund if canceled within 60 days of the event date.
Event rentals under $500, no refund if canceled within 30 days of the event date.
Event rentals of $500 and more, no refund if canceled within 60 days of the event date.
Event rentals under $500, no refund if canceled within 30 days of the event date.
A cancellation request must be made in writing to megan@autreymill.org.
How do I pay for my rental?
The invoice for your rental will be emailed to you by the Sales Director. The invoice can be viewed and paid online via HoneyBook. If you prefer not to pay online, you may also pay by cash or check. The non-refundable deposit of 25% is due upfront to reserve the date. Your final payment of 75% is required at least 30 days prior to your event. Please let the Sales Director know how you plan to pay.

What does Autrey Mill provide?
Autrey Mill only provides the venue space.
For an additional cost, you can rent tables and chairs. Tables and chairs are included if you purchase a package.
We do not provide linens.
The renter and caterer must supply all equipment necessary for any event. This includes linens, trashcan liners, utensils, dinnerware, etc. Deliveries and equipment setup or installation must be done on the day of the event and coordinated with the Autrey Mill Sales Director. Rental equipment must be picked up immediately following the event unless other arrangements have been pre-approved. Rental equipment pickups must be finished before 11 PM, or other arrangements must be scheduled before the event commencement. Questions? Email megan@autreymill.org
Can I serve alcohol at my rental?
Alcohol - Alcohol is permitted with the following requirements per the City of Johns Creek:
Permit set up through Autrey Mill Nature Preserve at an additional cost
JCPD Officer on-site set up through Autrey Mill Nature Preserve at an additional cost
Event liability insurance in the amount of $1,000,000 set up by the client. Autrey Mill recommends Wedsure Insurance. COI must be presented to Autrey Mill representative at least 30 days before the event.
All alcohol policies are created by the City of Johns Creek and are subject to change without notice.
Can I serve food? Do I have access to a kitchen? A grill?
Yes, you may serve food. If you will be using a vendor, please let the Event Coordinator know. Food trucks require additional licensing and inspections and need to be approved by Autrey Mill staff.
The only facility that includes a kitchen is the Summerour House. The rental of this space is required to use the kitchen. We do not provide cutlery or serving ware.
Where do I park?
We have two parking lots with a combined total of 35 spots plus 5 accessible spots. There is overflow parking near the Wildflower Trail. Parking on Old Alabama Road is not permitted. Overnight parking is not permitted and cars will be towed.
Are there any restrictions at Autrey Mill?
Within the interior of the buildings, you may not use tacks, tape, nails, or any adhesives on the walls/ woodwork. Please do not scatter real flower petals inside the Summerour house; only faux/silk petals are allowed inside. Confetti, glitter, candles, and sparklers are not permitted.
Guests may stay no later than 10 pm, while the reservationist and hosts may stay as late as 11 pm to clean up.
Autrey Mill is a smoke-free park. Smoking is prohibited.
You, as the renter, are responsible for cleaning up your rental. All items brought in by you, your vendors, or your guests must be removed. All of the tables should be cleared. The kitchen should be free of food and beverages and wiped down. After all the trash is gathered, the trash bags should be placed on the side of the road near the goat enclosure.
Can I set up a tent?
Please email us for more information.
What about park guests?
Autrey Mill is proud to host open grounds. We do not close in the event of a private event or rental. There will always be other park guests enjoying the preserve, but Autrey Mill staff will be available to redirect park guests if they take an interest in your rental.
