Scout Day 2023- Cancelled
Scout Day is Cancelled this year. Scout Day is a wonderful opportunity for both Cub Scouts and Girl Scouts to earn badges and achievements. Participants receive a “passport” that guides them through the various stations set up around the preserve where they can earn several badges. Activities designed for Cub Scouts, Daisies, Brownies, and Junior Girl Scouts. If you have any questions please call 678-366-3511 or email info@autreymill.org.
When is Scout Day 2023?
Scout Day 2023 HAS BEEN CANCELLED – We look forward to seeing all our Scout Partners in 2024!!
Price for Admission
$10 per scout. The $10 provides you access to Autrey Mill to complete multiple badges. The physical badges are not provided. The fee is charged for participating scouts and siblings. Parents and chaperones are free of charge. Hot chocolate and s’mores will be available to purchase for an additional cost.
Do we need to Register?
No prior registration is taken for the event. Payment is taken on the day of check-in. Cash, Venmo, and Credit Card accepted.
What badges are you offering on Scout Day 2023?
Activities are targeted at Cub Scouts (Tigers, Wolves, Bears, and Webelos) and Girl Scouts (Daisies, Brownies, and Jr. Girl Scouts).
Where do we park?
Autrey Mill does have parking lots on the property; however, parking is limited and we recommend carpooling.
