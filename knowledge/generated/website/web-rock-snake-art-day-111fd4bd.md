## Page content

Rock Snake Art
Add your painted rock at any point during Autrey Mill’s regular hours.
Find the tail, move it back, and place your own painted rock at the end!
Paint a rock at home and add it to our community snake sculpture! Our amazing sculpture is here to stay! You can add your painted rock to the snake at any time during Autrey Mill’s regular hours. Free to participate.
Instructions:
Find a rock
Decorate your rock with acrylic paint or paint markers at home.
Put your name and date on the bottom of your painted rock and place it at the tail end of the rock art snake!
Do not pick up anyone else’s rock.
All art styles and skill levels are welcome! Family-friendly artwork only.
Free to attend, donations greatly appreciated!
