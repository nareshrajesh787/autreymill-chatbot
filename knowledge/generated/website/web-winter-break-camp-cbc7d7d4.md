Seasonal Camps are always a favorite with a variety of Nature and Heritage themed lessons and programs! Each day of Camp is based on one of our popular Summer Camps and will include indoor/outdoor activities including theme-based hikes, crafts, and games. Sign up for one or more days to keep children engaged during the school break.
Schedule:
Monday, December 18th: Camp Rock and Roll Campers will explore the wonders of rocks and minerals and expand their knowledge of earth science. These future geologists will use rock identification kits and will break open a geode. This camp rocks!
Tuesday, December 19th: Camp Wonders of Wildlife Campers will get up close with some of our Amazing Animal Ambassadors! They will learn about their habitats, diet, and survival adaptations. This camp is wild!
Wednesday, December 20th: Camp Winter Wonderland Campers will explore the wonders of the winter season. They will make snow, learn about how our animals adapt to the cold, and enjoy some s’mores around the campfire! This camp is frosty fun!
Thursday, December 21st: Camp Arts and Crafts Campers will discover their inner artist while exploring different mediums such as painting, drawing, and sculpture. This camp is crafty!
Friday, December 22nd: Wonders of Wildlife Campers will get up close with some of our Amazing Animal Ambassadors! They will learn about their habitats, diet, and survival adaptations. This camp is wild!
