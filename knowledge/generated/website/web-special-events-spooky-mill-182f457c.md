## Page content

Autrey Mill Nature Preserve’s Halloween Fundraiser, Spooky Mill, will return on Saturday, October 10th, 2026, from 2 PM-9 PM!
Explore our beautiful nature preserve while enjoying carnival games, seasonal crafts, a haunted hike, exhibits, concessions, and more. Costumes are encouraged for all ages, and there are plenty of not-so-spooky surprises around every corner.
Perfect for families with young children (between 2 PM – 5 PM) and all ages from 5 PM – 9 PM, this event offers a festive way to celebrate the season in a safe and natural setting. Come celebrate Halloween with us—if you dare!
This is a family event designed for all ages. A parent or guardian must accompany children under 16 years of age.
Questions? Email info@autreymill.org.
