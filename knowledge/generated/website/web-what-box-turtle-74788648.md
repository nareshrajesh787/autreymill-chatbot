## Page content

Name: What
Species: Eastern Box Turtle
About: ‘What’ is a feisty box turtle with very bright orange skin and red eyes. He is hard to miss and he is always stirring up mischief if he’s not lounging in the mini pond. ‘What’ is always the center of attention and is the first to race out for dinner time. With that bright color, he has to have a bold personality to match! We call him ‘What” because we often find ourselves asking “What are you up to!?”.
