## Page content

Brought to you in partnership with Kaiser Permanente, Ageless Adventures is a series of programs designed for adults of all ages to connect with nature, nurture wellness, and enjoy community. From gardening and guided hikes to bird walks and yoga, there’s something for everyone who wants to stay active, engaged, and inspired. Explore the options below and join us for an adventure that’s bound to grow your connection with nature and leave your spirits refreshed.
Gentle Yoga with Joan
Support your balance, mobility, and peace of mind in a welcoming yoga class tailored for older adults. Joan’s gentle, mindful approach helps every participant feel strong, confident, and at ease.
