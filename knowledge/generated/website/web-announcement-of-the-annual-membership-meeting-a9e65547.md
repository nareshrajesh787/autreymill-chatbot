Annual Membership Meeting
You are invited to join the Autrey Mill Nature Preserve Association Board of Directors for the annual membership meeting on Tuesday, March 17th, 2026, at 6:30 p.m. The meeting agenda will include the election of members to the Board of Directors. All current members are invited to attend this brief meeting. Members present at the meeting, or by proxy, will elect five members to the Executive Board of Directors for one or two years.
When
Tuesday, March 17, 2026
6:30 PM
Where
Autrey Mill Nature Preserve
Summerour House
9770 Autrey Mill Road
Johns Creek, GA 30022
More Information:
Current membership is required to be eligible to vote or serve on the board. Those members in attendance at the meeting will have the opportunity to cast a vote for these candidates or to nominate candidates from the floor. If you’re unable to attend, you can return your proxy to ensure a legal selection of the board members. Click the button below to vote electronically, or click the following link to download, print, and mail/ turn in your vote at Autrey Mill Nature Preserve before 12 PM on Tuesday, March 17th, 2026. https://drive.google.com/file/d/1gGd44GKPFK8DzXv7qah9_gV7poJBs2TR/view?usp=sharing
