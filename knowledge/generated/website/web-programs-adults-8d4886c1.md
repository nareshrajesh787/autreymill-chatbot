## Page content

Critter Connections
Discover the fascinating world of animal enrichment with our Animal Ambassadors at Autrey Mill Nature Preserve! On select Tuesdays and Saturdays each month, join us for an unforgettable hour of fun, learning, and hands-on activities.
Rock Art Community Sculpture
Paint a rock and add it to our community snake sculpture! Our amazing sculpture is here to stay! You can add your painted rock to the snake at any time during Autrey Mill’s regular hours. Free to participate.
