Name: King
Species: Florida King Snake.
About King: He is a ten-year-old, 5’ft long, very mellow snake. King gets his name from his species but he also acts like royalty! He is incredibly relaxed and calm. He spends most of his time napping under his water bowl, or being the center of attention when the Visitor Center is open. His mellow nature is often humorous because he is always looking for the next nap session. If you join us for a Nature Birthday Bash, you may get to meet Mr. King!
