## Page content

Spring Break Camp
Spring Break Camp offers a variety of Nature, and Heritage themed exploration and crafts! Each day of Spring Break Camp is based on one of our favorite Summer Camps and will include indoor/outdoor activities including theme-based hikes, crafts, and games. Sign up for one or more days to keep children engaged during the school break.
9 am to 3 pm*: Camp. $63 per day or $55 for Family Members.
Amazing Arts and Crafts
Monday, April 3rd, 2023
Campers will explore their creativity by making a variety of arts and crafts! They will also take a step back in time through simple heritage crafts enjoyed in the past.
Sun, Moon, and Stars
Tuesday, April 4th, 2023
Campers will take part in a camp that is out of this world! They will learn about Earth, the Solar System, and all things in space!
Ecosystems Around the World
Wednesday, April 5th, 2023
Using a magnifying glass, campers will take a closer look at local wildlife and history. Participants will be engaged in crafts, animal encounters, historic tours, archaeological digs, scavenger hunts, puzzles, and much more. Campers will compare local wildlife to the amazing flora and fauna found all around the world!
Adventures Through History
Thursday, April 6th, 2023
This camp will transport explorers to various periods in history! We will set our time machine and explore life during the time of the Native Americans, Colonists, the Civil War era, the Industrial Revolution, the Depression, or the groovy 70s. Campers will discover these eras through engaging activities, games, and crafts from the past.
Rock and Roll
Friday, April 7th, 2023
This camp will let campers explore the wonders of native and unique rocks and minerals, and expand their knowledge of earth science. Campers get to experiment, explore, and play with themes that rock!
*Early and Aftercare are not offered for seasonal camps.
