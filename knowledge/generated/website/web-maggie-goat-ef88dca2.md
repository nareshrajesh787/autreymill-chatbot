Name: Maggie
Species: Nigerian Dwarf Goat
About Maggie: Maggie is the youngest of the goats and she is often teased by Cusco. Even though she is the youngest she always joins in the fun with the other girls and shows off that she isn’t just an underdog. Maggie is a huge fan of some good head scratches and will even do it for herself if you leave your hand out to her.
