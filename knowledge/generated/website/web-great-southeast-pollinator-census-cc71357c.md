Great Southeast Pollinator Census
On Saturday, August 23rd, 2025, from 10 AM – 12 PM, join Autrey Mill Nature Preserve, the North Fulton Master Gardeners, the University of Georgia Extension office, and 4 H for the Great Southeast Pollinator Census.
The Great Southeast Pollinator Census
Registration is not required; walk-ups are welcome. Anyone under 18 must have an adult guardian at the walk-up to sign the liability waiver. An adult must accompany anyone under 14 for the entire event.
Where:
Autrey Mill Nature Preserve
9770 Autrey Mill Rd.
Johns Creek, GA 30022
When:
Saturday, August 23rd, 2025
10 AM – 12 PM
What to wear:
Dress for the weather, wear sunscreen and bug spray, and bring a camping chair!
More information:
Check-in at the Autrey Mill Nature Preserve tent on the front lawn
All supplies will be provided: paper, identification guide, clipboard, pen, and chairs.
We will assign you to one of three pollinator gardens within the heritage village. The Summerour House Garden, The Butterfly Garden, or the new Hilltop Garden.
Bring a refillable water bottle – we have refill stations in front of the restrooms.
Plan to spend about 15 minutes in front of a pollinator plant.
Once your 15 minutes are up, bring your completed form back to the tent. One of our amazing volunteers will enter the data.
The Great Southeastern Pollinator Census is open to Georgia, South Carolina, North Carolina, and Florida residents.
If you can’t make it to Autrey Mill Nature Preserve, you can participate in your backyard. Visit https://gsepc.org/ to download the forms and instructions.
