Shakespeare In The Park
Autrey Mill Nature Preserve Association has partnered with Contemporary Classics Theatre for the fourth year to host one weekend of Shakespeare’s Richard II. Don’t wait; reserve your FREE tickets today!
Contemporary Classics Theatre presents the fourth annual Shakespeare in the Park! This year’s production is Richard II, featuring live music by Director Allen Cox.
Reserve your spot today so we can plan seating, address accessibility needs, and provide updates on weather or schedule changes. Your registration helps support local artists and keeps vibrant, affordable theatre in our community.
All performances are at 3PM.
Where:
Autrey Mill Nature Preserve
Amphitheatre Stage (In case of inclement weather, we will move to the open-air Pole Barn nearby)
9770 Autrey Mill Road
Johns Creek, GA 30022
When:
Saturday, April 25th, 2026 | 3 PM – audience seating will begin at 2:30 PM.
Sunday, April 26th, 2026 | 3 PM – audience seating will begin at 2:30 PM.
More Information:
Limited bench seating is available. Bring your stadium seat (with a back) or your lawn chair.
