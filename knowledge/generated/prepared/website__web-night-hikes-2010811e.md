On Demand Night Hikes
Learn about nocturnal wildlife while exploring the forests of Autrey Mill at night, then enjoy s’mores around the campfire! Bring a flashlight! Registration required. Children must be accompanied by a parent or guardian. If you are interested, please contact us to schedule a time! Please wear closed-toe shoes and dress appropriately for the weather. It is recommended you bring a water bottle.
45-minute experience hiking the trails and enjoying campfire s’mores
$150 program minimum, covers 10 participants
An additional $15 is charged for each extra participant, with a maximum of 25 participants.
Book a slot now!
Cancellation Policy:
Your date is not reserved until the program’s total cost is paid. If the cancellation request is sent at least two weeks before your scheduled program, 100% of the program fee will be refunded. Credit and debit card payments will be refunded to the original card.
If the refund request is sent less than two weeks prior to your program, you will receive an account credit or an opportunity to reschedule the program. A refund will not be offered. In the event of bad weather, you will be contacted to reschedule. All cancellation requests must be made in writing via email to info@autreymill.org.
