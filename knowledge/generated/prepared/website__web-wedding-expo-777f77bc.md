Wedding Expo 2022
Johns Creek Convention and Visitors Bureau and Autrey Mill Nature Preserve are proud to showcase Georgia businesses at the 2022 Johns Creek Wedding Expo. Boost your wedding business and connect to newly engaged couples who are planning to host their weddings in Johns Creek and the greater North Fulton area.
Are you getting married in the Johns Creek or Metro Atlanta area?
The Johns Creek Wedding Expo will feature multiple different vendors from the Johns Creek area! We will showcase vendors varying from local hotels to photographers to jewelry stores!
Interested in attending the expo? The expo is free to attend! No registration fees!
Click here to pre-register.
*Pre-registration not required to attend.
Date and Time:
March 26th, 2022
10am – 3pm
Location:
Autrey Mill Nature Preserve & Heritage Center
9770 Autrey Mill Road, Johns Creek, GA 30022
