## Approved page content

Autrey Mill Nature Preserve offers a variety of group programs that include engaging, hands-on learning activities and are consistent with the Georgia Educational Standards for grades K-8

All Grade Levels
Amazing Animals
Students will be introduced to our amazing Animal Ambassadors. Our wildlife instructors will provide education regarding animal adaptations, habitats, diet, and more. Program materials can be adapted to fit various age levels from Preschool to High School.
Wonders of Water
Students will learn about the importance of water in our environment. Our program educators will provide instruction on the water cycle and sources of water around the world. The Enviroscape will provide students with a hands-on interactive demonstration to the sources and effects of water pollution. Program materials can be adapted to fit various age levels from Preschool to High School.
Ocean Literacy Experience
In a new partnership with the Discover70 Foundation, AMNP is proud to offer an Ocean Literacy Experience designed around the UNESCO Ocean Literacy framework. Students explore how the ocean affects our lives, and how our actions affect the ocean through engaging, hands-on activities that connect local waterways to the global ocean. The program inspires students to better understand, value, and protect our ocean. Check out discover70.org for more info or click the link above to inquire about booking!
4th Grade and up
Georgia Gold
Catch gold fever at Autrey Mill! Students will learn about the 1800's Land Lottery and discover the mineral that shaped our state. Activities will include gold panning and a hike to our Autrey gold mine. This program is best suited for 4th grade and up.
Middle School and High School
Wildlife Rehabilitation
Older students will learn about the importance of wildlife rehabilitation- the act of taking in orphaned, injured, and ill wildlife in order to heal and return to the wild. Our Wildlife Rehabilitation team will demonstrate various medical equipment used in wildlife rehab and will provide instruction regarding how the students can become wildlife conservationists. This program is designed for Middle School or High School.
Adult Programs
Historical Homes
Travel back in time with one of Autrey Mill's docents to experience the history of our local area. See the restored homes, general store, and country church. Hear the stories of life on this land in the late 1800s and early 1900s. This program is designed for Adults.
