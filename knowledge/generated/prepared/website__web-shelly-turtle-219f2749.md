## Approved page content

Name: Shelly
Species: Yellow-bellied Slider
About: Shelly is a 5-year-old slider. She is the largest turtle in the Star Pond and the most mischievous! She came to us as a wild child with damage to her shell. With much love and a safer space to explore she has been living well. Even though she had some injuries she never let go of her mischievous ways. She is often found exploring all over the star pond enclosure, and even makes her way to Pebbles’s enclosure! Good thing she has Yertle and Crush to keep an eye on her.
