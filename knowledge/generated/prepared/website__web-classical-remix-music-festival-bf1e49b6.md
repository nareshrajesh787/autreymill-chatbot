Classical Remix Music Festival
Join us for a FREE outdoor concert at the gorgeous Autrey Mill Nature Preserve as you experience the beloved Bridgerton soundtrack performed live by a string quartet of Atlanta’s top musicians! To finish off the evening, Classical Remix musicians will be joined by the immensely talented students of the William Pu Music Academy, performing the incredible String Octet by Felix Mendelssohn: music people would have actually heard during the Bridgerton era. This concert is generously sponsored by Nancy Jiang.

Dress code: dressing like characters in Bridgerton is encouraged but optional! 🙂 The Music: Sections from the Bridgerton soundtrack, and Felix Mendelssohn’s Octet in E-flat Major, Op. 20. The Musicians: Featuring musicians from the Atlanta Symphony Orchestra, Kansas City Symphony, principal members of the Naples Philharmonic, ensemble vim, and more.
