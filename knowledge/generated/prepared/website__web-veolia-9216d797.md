Veolia
We are deeply grateful to Veolia for their continued support of Autrey Mill Nature Preserve and their commitment to protecting our local waterways. Through their partnership, Veolia helps power our Johns Creek cleanup efforts throughout and enables us to offer meaningful educational experiences that connect kids and families to the Chattahoochee River watershed.
With Veolia’s support, students and families are learning how everyday choices affect our water, how to conserve this vital resource, and how reducing pollution helps keep our watershed healthy for generations to come. These hands-on experiences inspire stewardship, curiosity, and a deeper respect for the natural world right in our own backyard.
Thank you, Veolia, for being a champion for clean water, environmental education, and a healthier community. Your investment is making a real difference, one stream, one family, and one lesson at a time.
