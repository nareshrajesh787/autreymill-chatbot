## Approved page content

Homeschool Adventures
Our homeschool curriculum provides an exciting learning experience that engages students in studying topics combining environmental studies, nature, heritage, and general science. Students will play games, make crafts, and explore the nature preserve! Children will be checked in at 10am, have a snack at 10:30am, and lunch at 12pm, with activities throughout. Checkout is at 2pm.
Offered Every Thursday – Registration Required
Ages 5-14 – 10 am to 2 pm | 10-14 $50.00 per session, or $45.00 for family-level members
