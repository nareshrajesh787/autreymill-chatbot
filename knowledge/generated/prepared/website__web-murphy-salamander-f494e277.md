Name: Murphy
Species: Tiger Salamander
About Scallion: Murphy is our Tiger Salamander. If you don’t see him at first, it means he is buried under the soil! As an amphibian, Murphy loves to be in a warm and moist environment, so he spends a lot of his time digging tunnels under the soil. Murphy’s favorite food is crickets!
