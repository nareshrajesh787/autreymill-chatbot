Critter Connections
Discover the fascinating world of animal enrichment with our Animal Ambassadors at Autrey Mill Nature Preserve! On select Tuesdays and Saturdays each month, join us for an unforgettable hour of fun, learning, and hands-on activities.
Your adventure begins in the Nature Classroom with a close-up encounter with a reptile or amphibian, where you’ll uncover amazing facts about the importance of enrichment. Then, dive into interactive activities like animal games, grooming, and creating play mats or mazes to help our ambassadors thrive.
But the excitement doesn’t stop there! Weather permitting, the group will head outdoors to meet another ambassador and take part in their enrichment, too. Every activity is designed to provide stimulation and joy for the animals while giving you a unique chance to connect with them.
Don’t miss this opportunity to learn, play, and make a difference in the lives of our incredible Animal Ambassadors. We can’t wait to see you there!
Offered on select Tuesdays and Saturdays
1:00 – 2:00 PM
All ages welcome – Children 16 and under must be accompanied by an adult
$15 for Family Members+ | $20 for non-members
