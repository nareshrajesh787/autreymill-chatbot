Creek Clean-up & Crawl
On Saturday, September 26th, 2026, join Autrey Mill Nature Preserve for the annual Johns Creek Crawl & Clean-up.
Click the button below to pre-register for the Johns Creek Crawl and Clean-up!
Registration is encouraged but not required; walk-ups are welcome. Anyone under 18 must have an adult guardian at the walk-up to sign the liability waiver. An adult must accompany anyone under 14 for the entire event.
Where:
The Doublegate neighborhood–Please park along Mt. Oglethorpe, Mt. Katadhin, Laithbank or other side street. Please DO NOT park on Twingate Drive, this will be our entry point into the creek and needs to remain clear. Click for Google Maps Pin
When:
Saturday, September 26th, 2026
9 AM – 12 PM
What to wear:
Water shoes, boots, or waders
All homeowners and their neighbors are encouraged to join us, or even look at streams/tributaries closer to home, to help. We will sort, bag, and weigh collected debris and litter for pickup the afternoon of the event, and the city will properly dispose of the waste.
More information:
Bring water shoes (if you’re getting in the water), a hat, a water bottle(reusable), sunscreen, and bug repellent.
Supplies already there will include water to refill your water bottle, a first aid kit, a cell phone (to take pictures and for a 911 call if needed), trash bags, gloves, and “grabbers”
We’ll do introductions and briefly review why it’s important to keep our watershed clean.
If you want to stay dry, let us know ahead of time. You can help by pulling invasive privet and/or removing heavy/full trash bags from those in the stream at key points, and providing replacement bags as needed.
Storm Water Credit:
Participating Johns Creek residents can use a 10% Stormwater credit to apply toward the Stormwater Fee next year. See the link below for more details. Examples of qualifying events include stream cleanups hosted by Autrey Mill Nature Preserve, Chattahoochee Riverkeepers, Keep North Fulton Beautiful, Rivers Alive, or other events that follow Adopt-A-Stream or Adopt-A-Road guidelines.
https://www.johnscreekga.gov/JCGA/Media/PDF-PW/Stormwater-Credit-Manual.pdf
Required documentation:
Submit a fee credit application, a screenshot or confirmation email of event registration, and a photograph of participation in the event.
