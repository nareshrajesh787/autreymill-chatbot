Thank You all for an AMAZING 2026 Summer!
What is Camp Autrey Mill?
Adventure Awaits at Camp Autrey Mill!
At Camp Autrey Mill, we believe summer should be magical, memorable, and full of wonder! Our unique camp experience is all about helping kids connect with nature, history, and wildlife in exciting, hands-on ways. Whether they're crafting, playing nature games, solving puzzles, or exploring real historical artifacts, every day brings something new to discover.
Campers will also get up close with live animals and learn why caring for our natural world matters—today and for the future! With small group sizes (just 10–12 campers per instructor), each child gets the attention they need to feel supported, curious, and confident as they explore the outdoors.
Nature. Discovery. Friendship. Fun.
Give your child a summer filled with fresh air, new friends, and unforgettable adventures. Join us at Camp Autrey Mill and let the journey begin!
Got questions? We’re happy to help—just send us an email!
Camper Code of Conduct
At Nature Camp, we want everyone to have fun, stay safe, and feel welcome. To make that happen, every camper is expected to follow this code of conduct every day:
Be Kind and Respectful
Use kind words and actions with everyone—campers, counselors, and animals too!
Listen when someone is talking and take turns when playing or speaking.
Keep hands, feet, and objects to yourself. No hitting, pushing, or rough play.
Be Safe
Stay with your group and follow directions from counselors.
Walk (not run!) on trails and keep your shoes on.
Use sticks and rocks for exploring—not for throwing.
Tell an adult right away if someone is hurt or if something doesn’t feel right.
Take Care of Nature
Treat all plants and animals gently—look with your eyes, not your hands.
Stay on marked trails and do not pick flowers or disturb wildlife.
Leave nature the way you found it. “Take only memories, leave only footprints.”
Be Ready to Explore
Come prepared each day with a water bottle, snack/lunch, sunscreen, and bug spray.
Use camp supplies and materials the right way and help clean up when you're done.
Try new things, ask questions, and have fun learning about the world around you!
Campers who have trouble following these rules will:
Be given a gentle reminder.
Be given time away from the group to cool down and reflect.
Have a counselor speak with their grown-up if the behavior continues.
In serious cases, a camper may be sent home for the day or dismissed from camp.
We’re here to have a great time together! Let’s make camp safe, fun, and full of adventure for everyone!

What should my camper bring and wear?
To help your child have a safe and comfortable day at Camp Autrey Mill, please make sure they come prepared with the following:
What to Wear:
Closed-toe shoes that are sturdy and good for hiking, walking, and playing outside
Comfortable clothing appropriate for the weather (we’ll be outdoors rain or shine!)
What to Bring:
A refillable water bottle (we have filtered water refill stations around camp)
A backpack or bag to bring their projects home and store clothes
A spare change of clothes is recommended for younger campers (ages 5–7)*
A plastic bag for wet or muddy clothes
Nut-free lunch (we are a nut-free camp for the safety of all campers)**
Morning snack
Extra snack if your camper is staying for Aftercare***
*Spills, mud, and fun happen—an extra outfit can be handy!
**Please help us keep all campers safe by avoiding foods with peanuts or tree nuts.
***Aftercare campers will need an additional snack to keep their energy up!
Let us know if you have any questions—we're here to help you get ready for a great camp experience!
Wild Water Camp- What to Bring
Get ready to splash, explore, and stay cool! If your camper is signed up for Wild Water Camp, please send them with the following each day:
A Towel and a plastic bag to bring it home in.
For the 5-7 year olds, you may want to have them arrive in a swim outfit, ready to play
Change of clothes for after-water activities
Water shoes (closed-toe preferred) for creek exploration—no flip-flops, please! - (old tennis shoes work in a pinch)
We’ll be getting wet and muddy and having a blast, so please pack accordingly! Let us know if you have any questions before your first day.
