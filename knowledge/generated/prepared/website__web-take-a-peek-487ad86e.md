Historic Homes
Join Autrey Mill Nature Preserve Staff and Board to step back in time and explore the rich history of Johns Creek at Autrey Mill’s Historic Home Day. This free community event offers a rare opportunity to tour several of Autrey Mill’s historic buildings, each hosted by knowledgeable docents ready to share their stories.
Tour four historic buildings:
Tenant Farmhouse
Summerour House
Green Store
⛪ Warsaw Church
Where:
Autrey Mill Nature Preserve
9770 Autrey Mill Road
Johns Creek, GA 30022
When:
Wednesday May 6th, 2026
10 AM – 2 PM
More Info:
Each building has an accessible ramp, but two are at the top of the drive. If you have mobility concerns, we suggest you tour the lower buildings first, then drive up to the upper parking lot to tour the buildings near the top of the driveway.
“These buildings hold the stories of the people who built Johns Creek,” says Lizen Hayes, Executive Director. “We’re excited to open them up and bring those stories to life.”
Autrey Mill will host Historic Home Days twice a year—once in the spring and once in the fall—offering ongoing opportunities for the public to connect with local history.
All ages welcome. Free admission—donations appreciated to support preservation.
