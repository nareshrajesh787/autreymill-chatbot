Thanksgiving Break Camp
Join us for three days of hands-on fun at our Thanksgiving Break Camps! Each themed day invites campers to explore, create, and give back to nature. From engineering silly inventions in Turkey Tech, to crafting animal treats in Forest Feasts, to spreading gratitude through Thankful for Nature, kids will enjoy a season of curiosity, creativity, and thankfulness at the Preserve.
