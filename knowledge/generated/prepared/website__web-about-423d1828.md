## Approved page content

Discover nature, history, and community at Autrey Mill
Explore wooded trails and wetlands, visit historic farmhouses, a country store, and a local church building relocated and restored to preserve the legacy of the farming community that once called this place home. Discover rescued animals and historic artifacts in our Visitor Center & Farm Museum. Become an Autrey Mill Nature Preserve Member and receice discounts on events, weekly and monthly programs or become a volunteer and docent.
What to expect
Visitor Center & Farm Museum
Our Visitor Center and Farm Museum combine living history with wildlife care. You’ll find rescued animals alongside historic tools, household items, and exhibits that illuminate everyday life on the farm.
Programs & events
We offer hands-on programs for children, interpretive hikes, volunteer opportunities, and senior-focused events. Many programs are free or low-cost—your visit and support help keep them accessible to the community.
How donations help
Your gifts directly support the preserve, our animals, and educational programming:
Animal care & habitat upkeep (food, veterinary care, enclosures)
Preservation and interpretation of historic buildings and artifacts
Free and low-cost programs for children, families, and seniors
Trail and habitat restoration and landscaping
Support Autrey Mill
Your donation keeps programs running, animals thriving, and historic buildings preserved for future generations. Thank you.
