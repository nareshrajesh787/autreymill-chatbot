Name: Ms. Fred
Species: White’s Tree Frog, Dumpy Tree Frog, Australian Green Tree Frog
About Ms. Fred: Fred is a mysterious lady, not only did it take a while to find out she was a miss, but also she is often found in the most peculiar positions from late-night antics. Whatever it may be that is she is doing- it always leaves us wondering what she gets herself into. She is 12 years old and one of our exotic species. This means she is one of the few species we have in the Visitor’s Center that is not native to the area!
