Sustainable Living
Looking for a way to responsibly take care of your household organic waste and reduce landfill? Come join Green Cell Inc. and Autrey Mill Nature Preserve Association to learn ‘How to Compost’. This interactive workshop will not only help you take the right steps to start composting but find a mentor to guide you through the process from start to finish.
