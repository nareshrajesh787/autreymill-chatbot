## Approved page content

9770 Autrey Mill Road
Johns Creek, GA 30022
Look for the Autrey Mill Nature Preserve sign on the north side of Old Alabama Road. Drive up the winding road and park in one of the parking areas near the historic buildings. Autrey Mill Nature Preserve can only be accessed via Old Alabama Road.
Visitors can listen to our cell phone audio tour to enhance their experience. The audio tour features information about our historic buildings and the preserve trails. These instructions are posted on the main kiosk at the parking lot.
