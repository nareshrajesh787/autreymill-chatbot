2017 Projects
24 Projects completed with 4502 Volunteer Hours
Sharavanan built a second Swing Arbor on the hill above the Butterfly Garden. It’s very popular with bird watchers and others wanting to sit back and relax as it faces the water reservoir.

Alex built an ‘Unnatural Corral’ on the Miller’s Trail, complete with an explanation board and answer sheet. We will use this in our education classes and camps, but visitors can also play this ‘find me’ game to find elements in the corral that should not belong in the natural environment.

David built a Crossover Bridge and Easel. The 12 planks of the bridge display the 12 points of the Scout Law. The Scout Law can be displayed on the easel and then the bridge can be “built” during a crossover ceremony. To make the bridge multi-functional, the opposite side of the planks have no words so any other non-Boy Scout group could use it. Boy Scout and other groups renting facilities at Autrey Mill can request use of the bridge during their rental.
