## Approved page content

Name: Ms.Jacobs
Species: Nigerian Dwarf Goat
About: Ms. Jacobs is a dwarf-dwarf goat. She has the smallest legs and can have some trouble because of them! She is small but mighty! You can find her often in the middle of playing with her sisters and still keeping up. She loves bonding with new friends and often does so by standing at your feet (but watch out for Cusco, she loves to be the center of attention).
