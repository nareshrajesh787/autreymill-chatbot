Nurture the Gardens
Join our beloved Garden Guru, Mr. Frank, or the North Fulton Master Gardeners (Wednesdays ONLY) for a hands-on and heartwarming gardening program designed especially for older adults. Together, we’ll nurture the beautiful Heritage, Summerour, and Pollinator Gardens at Autrey Mill while enjoying the fresh air, gentle exercise, and camaraderie of fellow garden enthusiasts.
Whether you’re a seasoned gardener or simply curious to learn, Mr. Frank and the North Fulton Master Gardeners will share tips, stories, and knowledge about the plants that support both our community and local wildlife. This opportunity is a wonderful way to stay active, connect with nature, and leave a lasting impact on the preserve.
All tools and guidance are provided—just bring your enthusiasm and love for the outdoors!
Offered on select Wednesdays, Fridays, and Saturdays
Wednesdays 9 AM – 10:30 AM
Friday and Saturday 10 AM-12PM and 12PM – 2PM
ALL Ages are welcome
Bring your own gardening gloves
