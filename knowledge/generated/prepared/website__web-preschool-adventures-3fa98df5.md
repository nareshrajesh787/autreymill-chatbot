## Approved page content

Preschool Adventures
Each adventure is unique and inspired by a theme that sparks a preschooler’s imagination. It includes a hands-on activity and craft. This “grown-up and me” program is for children ages 18 months to 6 years old. Grown-ups are required to stay for the whole program.
Offered during the traditional school year.
10 AM – 11 AM
Most Tuesdays
Ages 18 months to 6 years
Programs are rain or shine.
$12 for members, $15 for non-members.
Grown-ups are free with a participating Preschooler.
Click to Register
