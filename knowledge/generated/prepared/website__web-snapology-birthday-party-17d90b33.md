Snapology Birthday Party
We are so excited to announce our new Snapology Birthday Parties! Turn your child’s birthday into an unforgettable hands-on STEAM experience with LEGO® bricks, creativity, and technology.
THEMED BUILDING PARTY (Ages 3–10)
Creative hands-on building activities inspired by children’s favorite interests and themes. Children collaborate, build, experiment, and share their creations under instructor guidance.
SCIENCE OF SLIME PARTY (Ages 7–10)
Create slime and explore simple science concepts. Take-home activity included.
ROBOTICS PARTY (Ages 4–10)
Build, explore, and bring creations to life through hands-on robotics activities!
Children work individually or in small collaborative groups to build robotic models using LEGO®-based educational robotics systems and explore age-appropriate engineering concepts such as movement, sensors, mechanisms, and simple programming. Programs encourage creativity, teamwork, problem solving, and experimentation in a fun and engaging environment. Snapology programs are designed to adapt to different experience levels, making robotics accessible for both beginners and experienced builders.
