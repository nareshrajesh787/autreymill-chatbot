Cooking and Crafts
The Cooking and Crafts program will involve making delicious recipes and creative crafts! The themes and activities vary; so attend one or all. Topics may include; butter, ice cream, mini pies, hotcakes, and more! If the stove or oven is used it will be by the instructor. The children will only combine ingredients. 10 children per 1 instructor. This program will be taught by our Heritage Instructor Ms. Alison.
Each class stands on its own, so attend one, all, or several.
4:30pm – 6:00pm
Ages 5-11, Grades 1-5
$5 for members, $15 for non-members. Your Family Discount will be applied automatically in the cart.
Registration is Open!
*Cooking and Crafts is not licensed through Bright From the Start. Under Category 5 Cooking and Crafts is exempt from licensing. Category 5 Exemption: no more than four (4) hours a day, not to exceed eight (8) hours per week, cannot be combined with other categories for the same children attending. Children will be checked in promptly at 4:30 pm, they will make recipes until 5:30 pm and finish up with arts and crafts. Check out is at 6 pm.
