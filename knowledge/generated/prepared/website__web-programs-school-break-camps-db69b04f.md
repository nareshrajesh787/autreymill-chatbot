School Break Camp Autrey Mill
Camp Autrey Mill is not your “Run-of-the-mill” day camp! Camp Autrey Mill gives children the tools to safely explore the natural environment for a lifetime.
We know school breaks don’t always fit neatly into a family’s schedule, so our school break camps are flexible! Families can register for just one day, two days, or the entire week. Whether you need a day or two of childcare or want your camper to enjoy the whole adventure, we’d love to welcome them back to Autrey Mill this fall!
If you have any questions about Camp Autrey Mill, please email us!
Fall Break 2026 | Monday – Friday | 9AM – 3PM | September 21st – 25th
Sign up for one, two, three days, or the whole week!
$75 a day for non-members and $65 per day for Autrey Mill Nature Preserve Family Members+
Monday, September 21st | Camp Legendary
Campers will explore myths and legends from around the world while learning about legendary creatures like Bigfoot, Nessie, and dragons. Through games, hikes, crafts, storytelling, and imaginative activities, campers will discover how people have used stories to explain the mysteries of nature and the world around them.
Tuesday, September 22nd | Falling Waters
Dive into the amazing world of water! Campers will explore oceans, creeks, and the water cycle through hands-on STEM challenges, animal games, creek hikes, boat races, conservation activities, and creative experiments that show why water is so important.
Wednesday, September 23rd | Fall Festival
Celebrate the fun of fall with a full day of outdoor games, creative activities, and friendly competition! Campers will enjoy obstacle courses, kite and airplane challenges, garden games, parachute play, relays, and plenty of opportunities to run, play, and explore.
Thursday, September 24th | Fall for Wildlife Wonders
Get wild as campers discover the fascinating animals that live around us! Through animal encounters, field exploration, games, hikes, and hands-on activities, campers will learn about wildlife, animal adaptations, habitats, and the signs animals leave behind.
Friday, September 25th | Creature Crafts
Let creativity run wild! Campers will combine art and nature to create imaginative animal crafts, explore patterns and textures on a nature hike, and enjoy hands-on projects using pinecones, leaves, yarn, and other natural materials.

What is Camp Autrey Mill?
Adventure Awaits at Camp Autrey Mill!
Our unique camp experiences are all about helping kids connect with nature, science, history, and wildlife in exciting, hands-on ways. Whether they're crafting, playing nature games, solving puzzles, or exploring real historical artifacts, every day brings something new to discover.
Got questions? We’re happy to help—just send us an email!
Camper Code of Conduct
At Nature Camp, we want everyone to have fun, stay safe, and feel welcome. To make that happen, every camper is expected to follow this code of conduct every day:
Be Kind and Respectful
Use kind words and actions with everyone—campers, counselors, and animals too!
Listen when someone is talking and take turns when playing or speaking.
Keep hands, feet, and objects to yourself. No hitting, pushing, or rough play.
Be Safe
Stay with your group and follow directions from counselors.
Walk (not run!) on trails and keep your shoes on.
Use sticks and rocks for exploring—not for throwing.
Tell an adult right away if someone is hurt or if something doesn’t feel right.
Take Care of Nature
Treat all plants and animals gently—look with your eyes, not your hands.
Stay on marked trails and do not pick flowers or disturb wildlife.
Leave nature the way you found it. “Take only memories, leave only footprints.”
Be Ready to Explore
Come prepared each day with a water bottle, snack/lunch, sunscreen, and bug spray.
Use camp supplies and materials the right way and help clean up when you're done.
Try new things, ask questions, and have fun learning about the world around you!
If Rules Are Not Followed | Campers who have trouble following these rules will:
Be given a gentle reminder.
Be given time away from the group to cool down and reflect.
Have a counselor speak with their grown-up if the behavior continues.
In serious cases, a camper may be sent home for the day or dismissed from camp.
We’re here to have a great time together! Let’s make camp safe, fun, and full of adventure for everyone!
Camp Schedule & Hours
Camp Hours
Drop-Off: 9:00 AM – 9:15 AM*
Pick-Up: 3:00 PM – 3:15 PM**

**If you arrive later than 3:15 PM for pick-up, please proceed to the Visitor Center.
***We have a 15-minute grace period for pickup, each minute after this period will incur a $10 charge per minute.
What should my camper bring and wear?
To help your child have a safe and comfortable day at Camp Autrey Mill, please make sure they come prepared with the following:
What to Wear:
Closed-toe shoes that are sturdy and good for hiking, walking, and playing outside
Comfortable clothing appropriate for the weather
What to Bring:
A refillable water bottle (we have filtered water refill stations around the property)
A backpack or bag to bring their projects home and store clothes
A spare change of clothes is recommended for younger campers (ages 5–7)*
A plastic bag for wet or muddy items
Nut-free lunch (we are a nut-free camp for the safety of all campers)**
Morning snack
*Spills, mud, and fun happen—an extra outfit can be handy!
**Please help us keep all campers safe by avoiding foods with peanuts or tree nuts.
Let us know if you have any questions—we're here to help you get ready for a great camp experience!
What are the costs?
The Family Membership price is $65 per day; the non-member price is $75 per day.
If you are new to Autrey Mill and would like to purchase a Family Membership and a day or two of School Break camp, Good news! The new system will automatically apply discounts to your programs in the same transaction!
How to Register
Create an account in our registration system, Cogran.
Add your child(ren) as a participant.
Choose the camp days you’d like to register for.
Proceed to checkout!
Prefer to pay by cash or check? Please email lizen.hayes@autreymill.org —we're happy to help!
Cancellation & Changes
Cancel two or more weeks before camp: receive an 80% refund
Cancel within two weeks of camp: We will add a credit to your Autrey Mill registration account ONLY, no refund
Want to switch camp days instead of canceling? If space is available, we’re happy to help!
