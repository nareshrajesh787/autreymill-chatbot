You’re Invited to Our Second Annual Ghosts & Goblins Gala!
Step into an evening of eerie elegance at our Second Annual Ghosts & Goblins Gala, held in a stunning Victorian-style mansion with a haunted past. Join us on Saturday, October 24, 2025, from 6:00 to 8:00 PM for a night of chilling charm and spirited fun, exclusively for adults.
Enjoy heavy hors d’oeuvres, a selection of wine and beer, and a hauntingly delicious specialty cocktail, all served in an atmospheric setting that promises beauty and a few goosebumps. As you mix and mingle in your most bewitching attire, you’ll also have the chance to bid on unique treasures during our silent auction—just beware of any ghosts with competing offers!
Tickets are $50 per person or $85 per couple, with all proceeds supporting our ongoing community programs. Don’t miss your chance to experience the glamour and the goosebumps of this one-of-a-kind gala! Attire: Cocktail or Costumes.
Tickets are available now!
