## Approved page content

Family Night Hikes
Learn about Georgia native wildlife while exploring the forests of Autrey Mill. Then join us by the campfire to make s’mores. Bring a flashlight! Our night hike program is offered year round as an on-demand program for families!
Historic Building Tours
Join us when our heritage staff and docents are on-site to take a quick peek at our building collections and hear about our preservation and research efforts.
