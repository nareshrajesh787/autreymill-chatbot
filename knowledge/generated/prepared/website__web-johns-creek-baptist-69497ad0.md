Johns Creek Baptist
A heartfelt thank you to Johns Creek Baptist Church, whose 2nd annual JCBC Jaunt 5k and 1-mile Fun Run raised over $7,000 for the Autrey Mill Nature Preserve Summer Camp Scholarship Fund for 2026.
Autrey Mill Nature Preserve’s unique camps are dedicated to providing a one-of-a-kind experience that equips children with the tools to explore and appreciate the natural environment safely and for a lifetime. Our team is passionate about instilling an appreciation for history and nature through a diverse range of activities, including crafts, games, puzzles, and historical artifacts. Children will also have the opportunity to meet and learn about various animals and the importance of wildlife and nature preservation. Our camp ratio of 10-12 campers per 1 instructor ensures that your child receives the personalized attention they deserve. If you would like to view our current offerings for Summer Camp 2026, please click HERE or visit https://autreymill.org/programs/summer-camp/
